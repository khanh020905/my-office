# Car models

`cars.json` holds the city's everyday cars, made by `scripts/build-cars.mjs` from Kenney's
[Car Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit) 3.1 (CC0 1.0: free for any use; credit is welcome, not required). Each
model is split into its paint (painted per car) and the rest (tinted glass, lights, bumpers, the kit's wheels), turned
nose along +x and scaled ×1.2.

The kit's models stand in for the cars Vietnam drives: sedan for the Toyota Vios and Hyundai Accent, sedan-sports for
the Honda City RS, hatchback-sports for the Kia Morning, suv for the Toyota Fortuner, suv-luxury for the VinFast VF8
(with VinFast's light bar added), truck for the Ford Ranger, van for the Mitsubishi Xpander, delivery for a box van,
taxi for the green and white taxi companies, ambulance and firetruck for the 115 ambulance and the 114 fire engine. The VinFast VF3 and the Xanh SM VF e34 are made in
`src/office/world/backdrops/street-life.js`. No maker's logo is drawn on any of them.
