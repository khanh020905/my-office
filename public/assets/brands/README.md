# Shop signs and liveries

The convenience-store logos on the shops in the city backdrop (src/office/world/backdrops/shops.js), and the
ride-hailing logos on the taxis and riders' jackets (street-life.js). Each file is
unchanged from its source: Wikimedia Commons marks them public domain as text logos (PD-textlogo): too simple to be
protected by copyright. They are still trademarks of their owners. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by
any of them, and the logos are shown only as part of a street scene.

Which chains appear depends on the city picked for the weather, as of October 2026: Ho Chi Minh City has GS25, Circle K
and 7-Eleven (almost all of 7-Eleven's Vietnamese stores are there); Hanoi has Circle K, GS25 and WinMart+; Đà Nẵng has
WinMart+ (no Circle K, GS25 or 7-Eleven could be confirmed there) next to the city's own food: mì Quảng and seafood.
